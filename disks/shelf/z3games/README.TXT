FREE Z3 GAMES
=====================================

Play one:     VEZZA MOONGLOW.Z3
Save and restore inside the game with SAVE and RESTORE (give a file
name). QUIT leaves the game.

MOONGLOW.Z3  Moonglow, Miniventure #1, by Dave Bernazzani (release 3).
CATSEYE.Z3   Cat's Eye, Miniventure #2, by Dave Bernazzani (release 3).
             Two short ones: a good place to start.
FANTASY.Z3   The Fantasy Dimension, a PunyJam 3 game by Johan Berntsson
             (release 2).
SUBSAB.Z3    Submarine Sabotage, by Garry Francis (release 3).

Each is listed as freeware in the Interactive Fiction Database and is
kept in the IF Archive (ifarchive.org/if-archive/games/zcode/), which
takes only works that may be freely distributed. They are unchanged.
Their authors keep their rights: they may be shared, not sold.

VEZZA.COM    Vezza, a Z-machine interpreter for CP/M by Shawn Sijnstra,
             the vezza-C2 build (80x24, VT100), from
             gitlab.com/sijnstra1/vezza. GPL-2.0-or-later: see VEZZA.LIC.
